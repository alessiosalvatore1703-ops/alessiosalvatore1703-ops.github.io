\subsection*{Research Projects}
\textbf{Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robots Control} \faVideo\ \href{https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oPd9mkxEaO8}{Video} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/aerial-knowledge-distillation/ReportPLR_Salvatore_Stefanini.pdf}{Report}\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Autonomous Systems Lab -- Roland Siegwart Lab} (selected among 150 applicants for a 30-student cohort)
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Model-based controllers for an overactuated tilt-rotor MAV (OMAV, 10 actuators) rely on disturbance observers to handle model mismatch and external forces, but observer bandwidth, measurement noise, and tuning limit how fast they adapt
    \item Replaced it with a single recurrent LSTM policy distilling parameter-matched expert controllers over 30,000 randomized plants, trained closed-loop through differentiable vehicle dynamics on $\sim$42 h of simulated flight
    \item In simulation the policy outperforms the nominal model-based controller in velocity tracking ($p < 10^{-10}$, 2,000 paired rollouts) and cuts divergence under $\pm$30\% model mismatch from 41.5\% to 24\%; deployed zero-shot on the OMAV
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/task-aware-3d-mapping/3dv_final_report.pdf}{Report}
\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Computer Vision and Geometry Group -- Marc Pollefeys Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item A robot mapping a building at uniform voxel resolution spends most of its memory on regions irrelevant to the task it was sent to do; framed online per-region resolution allocation (split/keep/merge) as an RL problem under an explicit memory budget
    \item Re-implemented PEANUT's dense voxel map as a sparse hierarchical multi-resolution grid (5/10/20 cm; Habitat, HM3D) and trained a PPO policy acting through two learned score thresholds, sidestepping an intractable per-block action space
    \item The learned policy retains 79\% of a uniformly fine map's point-goal navigation success with 87\% less memory (20 unseen episodes), matching an all-coarse map's memory footprint at far higher success
\end{itemize}
