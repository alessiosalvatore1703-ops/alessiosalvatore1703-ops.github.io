\subsection*{Research Projects}
\textbf{Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robots Control} \faVideo\ \href{https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oPd9mkxEaO8}{Video} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/aerial-knowledge-distillation/ReportPLR_Salvatore_Stefanini.pdf}{Report}\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Autonomous Systems Lab -- Roland Siegwart Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Multi-teacher distillation for the OMAV, an overactuated tilt-rotor MAV: each of 30,000 randomized simulated vehicles is flown by its own parameter-matched model-based expert, and the full family of experts is compressed into a single LSTM policy -- trained closed-loop by backpropagating through the differentiable vehicle dynamics (BPTT, $\sim$42 h of simulated flight)
    \item Outperforms the nominal model-based controller in velocity tracking (2,000 paired rollouts) and nearly halves its divergence rate under $\pm$30\% model mismatch, approaching the parameter-matched teachers; deployed zero-shot on the real OMAV
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/task-aware-3d-mapping/3dv_final_report.pdf}{Report}
\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Computer Vision and Geometry Group -- Marc Pollefeys Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Dense 3D maps waste memory on detail the task never uses -- trained a PPO agent that decides, region by region and online during navigation, where the map needs fine voxels and where coarse ones suffice, under a hard memory budget
    \item On unseen scenes the learned map keeps 79\% of point-goal navigation success with 87\% less memory than a uniformly fine map (20-episode validation); built as a sparse hierarchical multi-resolution rewrite of PEANUT's voxel map (Habitat, HM3D)
\end{itemize}
