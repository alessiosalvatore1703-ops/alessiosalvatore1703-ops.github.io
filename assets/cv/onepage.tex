\subsection*{Research Projects}
\textbf{Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robots Control} \faVideo\ \href{https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oPd9mkxEaO8}{Video} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/aerial-knowledge-distillation/ReportPLR_Salvatore_Stefanini.pdf}{Report}\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Autonomous Systems Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Model-based controllers for an overactuated drone (OMAV) lose accuracy when the vehicle drifts from its model, and disturbance observers can be too slow to compensate
    \item Distilled multiple expert controllers, each tuned to a different version of the vehicle, into a single recurrent neural controller that adapts from its recent flight history: in simulation it tracks velocity better and diverges far less under $\pm$30\% model errors (41.5\% $\rightarrow$ 24\%), and it flew the OMAV without retraining
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/task-aware-3d-mapping/3dv_final_report.pdf}{Report}
\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Computer Vision and Geometry Group}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item A robot mapping a building at uniform voxel resolution spends most of its memory on regions irrelevant to its task
    \item Trained a PPO agent reallocating resolution per region online under a memory budget, on a sparse hierarchical grid in Habitat/HM3D: it keeps 79\% of a uniformly fine map's point-goal success with 87\% less memory on 20 unseen episodes
\end{itemize}
