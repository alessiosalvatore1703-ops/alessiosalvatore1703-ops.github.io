\subsection*{Research Projects}
\textbf{Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robots Control} \faVideo\ \href{https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oPd9mkxEaO8}{Video} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/aerial-knowledge-distillation/ReportPLR_Salvatore_Stefanini.pdf}{Report}\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Autonomous Systems Lab -- Roland Siegwart Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Multi-teacher distillation for the OMAV, an overactuated tilt-rotor MAV: each randomized simulated vehicle is flown by its own parameter-matched expert, and the whole expert family is compressed into one LSTM policy trained closed-loop through differentiable dynamics with DAgger on $\sim$42 h of simulated flight
    \item The student beats its teachers: better velocity tracking than the nominal expert (2,000 paired rollouts) and nearly half the divergence under $\pm$30\% model mismatch; deployed zero-shot on the real OMAV
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL} \faFilePdf\ \href{https://alessiosalvatore1703-ops.github.io/assets/projects/task-aware-3d-mapping/3dv_final_report.pdf}{Report}
\hfill Feb--Jun 2026\\
\textit{Computer Vision and Geometry Group -- Marc Pollefeys Lab}
\begin{itemize}
    \vspace{-5pt}
    \item Dense 3D maps waste memory on detail the task never uses -- trained a PPO agent that decides online, region by region, where the map needs fine voxels and where coarse ones suffice, under a hard memory budget
    \item On unseen scenes the learned map keeps 79\% of point-goal success with 87\% less memory than a uniformly fine map (20-episode validation); built as a sparse hierarchical rewrite of PEANUT's voxel map (Habitat, HM3D)
\end{itemize}
