Feb — Jun 2026 · 3D Vision · CVG, Marc Pollefeys Lab

Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL

I extended the PEANUT object-goal navigation pipeline so the robot's 3D map spends its memory where the task needs it: a sparse hierarchical hash-grid holds three coexisting resolutions, and a PPO policy splits and merges blocks online under a hard memory budget. At a scarce 8 MB budget, task-aware allocation doubled navigation success (0.25 → 0.50 on validation episodes) over memory-only allocation.

Feb — Jun 2026 · Perception & Learning for Robotics · ASL, Roland Siegwart Lab

Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robot Control

I distilled an expensive model-based controller for an over-actuated omnidirectional MAV (5 tiltable rotor arms, 10 actuators) into lightweight recurrent policies for onboard deployment: 48,000 GPU-batched expert rollouts with parameter randomization, LSTM/GRU students trained closed-loop through a differentiable dynamics model, and an explicit DAgger loop relabeling student-visited states with the compiled expert.
