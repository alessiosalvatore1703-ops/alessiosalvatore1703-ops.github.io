Featured Work

Feb — Jun 2026 · Perception & Learning for Robotics · ASL, Roland Siegwart Lab

Knowledge Distillation for Aerial Robot Control

Omnidirectional aerial vehicles can fly and orient in any direction, but the model-based controllers behind this are computationally heavy and need careful tuning for each vehicle. The goal of this project is to compress that expertise into a small neural network able to run on the drone itself. Trained by imitation across thousands of simulated flights, the resulting policy controls the real OMAV in real time and stays reliable even when the vehicle differs from its model.

Feb — Jun 2026 · 3D Vision · CVG, Marc Pollefeys Lab

Goal-Driven Adaptive-Resolution 3D Mapping with RL

3D maps are essential to robotic systems for tasks like navigation and manipulation, but their memory cost grows quickly with scene size and detail. The goal of this project is a quality-adaptive mapping system that reconstructs in high quality only the parts of the scene relevant to the task at hand. Built on MapAdapt and PEANUT, a learned goal-conditioned policy decides where detail is worth its cost — the resulting map keeps 79 % of navigation success with 87 % less memory than a uniformly fine one.
